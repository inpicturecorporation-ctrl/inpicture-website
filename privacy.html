Privacy Policy

Inpicture · Last updated 20 October 2026

Your work stays on your device

Inpicture stores editable projects and interface preferences locally. Selected images are accessed when you choose to import or edit them. Auto Mask and Upscale process images on the device. This release has no computer-helper connection, image-generation service, account system, advertising or analytics integration.

Saving, export and deletion

Exports are saved to device media folders or the location you choose. Deleting an in-app project does not delete exported copies. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes app-local projects and settings; keep backups of artwork you want to retain. Exported files remain under your control.

Camera and external links

If you choose camera import, Android opens your camera app. External links, including Instagram and email, open another application whose privacy practices apply. Inpicture does not silently send artwork to those destinations.

Contact and support messages

You may email thomas.thanos@hotmail.com. We receive the address, text and attachments you choose to send. We use support messages to respond and troubleshoot. Messages are retained while needed to resolve the request and for related support records; request deletion by email. Applicable legal obligations may require retention.

Children

No advertising or tracking is built into this release. Do not email unnecessary personal information about children.

Website

This static website uses no analytics, advertising, cookies or contact forms. This website is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information, including IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and secure the website. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement.

Changes

We will update this policy when app behaviour changes. Questions and deletion requests: thomas.thanos@hotmail.com.
